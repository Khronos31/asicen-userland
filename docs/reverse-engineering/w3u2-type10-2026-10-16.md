## Observation and correction

The previous guard accepted only decoded controller type 0x0f.
The W3U2 at USB paths 1-3.1 / 1-3.2 returned controller 4a/09 raw 0x20,
decoded as 0x10 by (raw & 0x3e) >> 1. Firmware upload and runtime
0b06:0004 enumeration succeeded, but the old guard exited 3 before ready.

The shared predicate now accepts type 0x10 only for the canonical W3U2 profile.
Both powered initialization and link diagnostics use it. Other models retain
the type 0x0f boundary. Revision, I2C status/length, output-idle and firmware
identity checks remain unchanged.

## Limits

Physical capacity is 2T+2S; the current operational backend exposes primary
receiver0:S and receiver1:T with one active lease. Secondary reception,
simultaneous reception, sustained card operation and live removal/reinsertion,
LNB-on electrical behavior, other
channels, long-duration endurance and other platforms remain unverified here.
Loader1738:5211 alone cannot distinguish the paired models; explicit model
selection and firmware fingerprint checks remain required.

These results do not certify the electrical health of the user's board or its
D15 component. They do not establish a W3U3 drop fix or replace release gates.
